⚙ SETTINGS
🎬 CLIP RECORDING
On by default so the clip button always works. Records the last 20 seconds in your browser. Press G (keyboard) or click the right stick (controller) to save a clip. PC only — off on phones. Runs on your device, never on the game server. Turn it off if you want every last frame of performance.
🎬 AUTO WIN-CLIP
Off by default. Turn it on and your cash-out is auto-recorded and handed to SHARE MY WIN — one tap to post it to TikTok / X / Instagram. Only records during the cash-out circle, so it never affects gameplay.

MIC SENSITIVITY —
VOICE VOLUME —
Echo when you're also in Discord? Your browser can only cancel its own sound, not Discord's — so Discord comes out your speakers, back in your mic, and everyone hears it twice. Push-to-talk plus a lower sensitivity (slider right) cut most of it. Headphones remove it completely.
Your mic and what you hear are separate: muting yourself never silences anyone else.
GRAPHICS
Same game either way — everything is drawn in the same place. Lower settings just drop the extra polish (glowing rings, pickup sparkles, segment shading) for more FPS.

💬 CHAT COMMANDS
Press T to open chat, type the command, hit Enter. The amount is always last, in US dollars.
CASH OUT / DIE
/bet EMINEM 1
$1 that EMINEM cashes out — banks their money instead of dying.
/bet against EMINEM 1
$1 that EMINEM dies instead.
SURVIVE A TIMER
/survive EMINEM 1
$1 that EMINEM is still alive after 60s (60s is the default).
/survive EMINEM 30s 1
Same, but 30 seconds. Allowed: 30s · 60s · 120s.
/survive against EMINEM 30s 1
$1 that they don't last 30 seconds.
ONE SNAKE vs ANOTHER
/outlast EMINEM SNAKEPOT 1
$1 that EMINEM lasts longer than SNAKEPOT. Neither has to be you.
/outlast against EMINEM SNAKEPOT 1
The other side — SNAKEPOT lasts longer.
/duel EMINEM 1
$1: your snake vs EMINEM. Settles only on a decisive kill between you two.
TAKE SOMEONE ELSE'S BET
/bets
Lists the open bets, numbered. Your own show as (yours).
/accept 2
Takes bet #2 from that list — you put up the same stake on the other side. /take works too.
/accept
With no number, takes the only open bet — if there's exactly one.
/help
This panel. F8 opens the full betting panel with buttons.
HOW THE MONEY WORKS
  • Even money. Your $1 against their $1 — the winner takes about $1.84 (8% fee).
  • The fee only applies to matched bets. If nobody takes the other side you get 100% back, no fee.
  • Minimum bet is $0.50.
  • Names are partial and case-insensitive — /bet emin 1 finds EMINEM. Names with spaces work too.
  • You can back your own snake to win — never to lose. /bet against yourself is blocked, and so is any bet that pays out when your snake loses.
  • Anything that isn't a command is just sent as a normal chat message.
WHEN MONEY IS ON YOU
You'll see it live: 💚 someone bet ON you or 🎯 someone bet AGAINST you, and again when an opponent matches it.
