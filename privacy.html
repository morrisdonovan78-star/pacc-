Privacy Policy

2. Voice chat

Voice chat is optional and off until you enable it. When enabled, your microphone audio is relayed through our game servers to other players in your lobby in real time. We do not record or store voice audio on our servers.

Clips can capture voice. The optional clip feature records the last 20 seconds of gameplay in your browser, including in-game voice chat, and — when enabled — uploads it to a public Discord channel. That means what you and other players say in voice chat can end up in a public clip. Clips are off by default. You can disable them entirely, or disable just your own microphone in clips, in Settings.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect or store card numbers or bank details. Where a card payment option is offered, it is operated by a third-party provider and payment details go directly to them.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or advertising trackers.

4. Who we share with

  • Authentication provider — verifies your email at sign-in.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — serve the site and run the game servers.
  • Solana blockchain — deposits, payouts and wagers settle on a public ledger. On-chain transactions are public and permanent, and cannot be deleted by us or by you.
  • Payment providers — if you choose to buy or sell cryptocurrency with a card, that provider handles the transaction and any identity verification under its own policies.
  • Discord — only if you enable clips, in which case the clip is posted to a public channel.
  • Other players — your display name, snake colour, score and live position are visible in the arena; your name and results may appear on public leaderboards.
  • Law enforcement — where we are legally required to disclose.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage for your session, wallet, and settings. These are necessary for the game to function; there are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Retention

  • Account and gameplay records are kept while your account is open and for a reasonable period afterwards for security, dispute resolution and legal obligations.
  • Voice audio is not retained.
  • Clips you publish remain in the Discord channel until removed there.
  • On-chain transactions are permanent and outside our control.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond within the period required by applicable law. Note that we cannot delete on-chain records, and we may need to retain limited data where we have a legal obligation to do so.

8. Children

SNAKE POT is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to account data. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for keeping your sign-in credentials and device safe. Anyone with access to your account can move your funds.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
