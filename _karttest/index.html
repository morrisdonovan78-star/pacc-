KART ARENA — Mode 7

Drift (SHIFT) charges your reserve. SPACE burns it for speed. Q dives at the car ahead and E shuts the door on the car behind — both spend the SAME reserve, and both do nothing at all unless someone is actually there, so you cannot use either as a free boost. GRIP wears as you lean on the car and comes back when you drive smoothly.

PvP wager racing on today’s circuit, live on the Chicago and Frankfurt servers. 2–8 players, everyone on the same track in the same kart — no rubber-banding, no items. Winner takes the pot minus 10%; ties split.

Drift is the skill core: hold SHIFT through a corner while steering to charge — blue, then orange, then purple — and release to pour that charge into your boost reserve. Hold SPACE to burn the reserve a bit at a time; what you don't spend, you keep. Steer the other way mid-drift to flick the drift across without letting go.
